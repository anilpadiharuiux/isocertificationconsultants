# Brand Guide

> Voice, tone, target audience, key messaging, and competitor positioning for ISO Certification Consultant.

---

## Brand Identity

| Element | Value |
|---------|-------|
| **Company** | ISO Certification Consultant |
| **Tagline** | ISO Certification Consulting for Canadian Manufacturers |
| **Model** | Hybrid — AI-powered platform AND expert consultants working together |
| **Delivery types** | `"app"`, `"consultant"`, `"hybrid"` — never `"person"` |

### What ISO Certification Consultant IS
- An ISO certification consulting firm
- AI-powered compliance platform
- Expert consultants for Canadian manufacturers
- A hybrid of technology and human expertise

### What ISO Certification Consultant is NOT
- Not a software-only product
- Not a certification body (we prepare clients for certification, we don't issue certificates)

### Legal entity
- Operated by **ISO Certification Consultant Inc.** (Canadian company). ISO Certification Consultant Inc. appears as the legal entity on `/privacy`, `/terms`, and the site-wide JSON-LD `legalName` field. Site operates under Canadian law (PIPEDA for privacy; Copyright Act (Canada) for IP; governing law and jurisdiction: Province of [Province], Canada).

## Target Audience

### Primary Personas

| Persona | Title | Industry | Pain Points |
|---------|-------|----------|------------|
| **Quality Manager** | Quality Manager / QA Director | Manufacturing, Automotive, Aerospace | Documentation burden, audit prep, maintaining compliance |
| **Compliance Officer** | Compliance / Regulatory Affairs | Medical Devices, Food & Beverage | Regulatory complexity, multi-standard management |
| **Operations Executive** | VP Operations / Plant Manager | Manufacturing, Construction | Customer requirements, supply chain demands, cost of non-compliance |
| **Owner/GM** | Owner / General Manager | SME Manufacturing | Winning contracts, competitive advantage, don't know where to start |

### Geographic Focus
- **Primary:** Canada (Ontario focus — SW Ontario + Golden Horseshoe)
- **Secondary:** United States (manufacturing states)
- **City targets:** Toronto, Hamilton, Mississauga, Windsor, London, Kitchener-Waterloo, Guelph, Oshawa, Brampton, Markham, St. Catharines, Barrie

### Company Size
- Small manufacturers (under 50 employees)
- Mid-size operations (50-500 employees)
- Regional fabricators, family-owned operations, growing production facilities
- **Avoid:** "small/mid-size manufacturers" (use specific alternatives above)

## Voice & Tone

### Voice Attributes
- **Expert but accessible** — deep ISO knowledge delivered plainly
- **Confident, not salesy** — authority without pressure
- **Practical, not theoretical** — real-world manufacturing focus
- **Direct** — 2-4 sentences per response in chatbot; concise throughout

### Writing Style Rules
- North American English ("z" spellings: organization, customize, optimize)
- Never British/Canadian spellings (no organisation, colour, labour)
- Zero first person (we/our/I) in mega articles
- Third person throughout blog content
- No "Pro Tips" labels, no "Phase" labels
- Maximum 150 tokens per chatbot response

### Banned Phrases
| Banned | Use Instead |
|--------|------------|
| "small/mid-size manufacturers" | "shops under 50 employees", "regional fabricators", "family-owned operations", "growing production facilities" |
| "from scratch/ground up" | *(remove entirely)* |
| "documentation burden/maturity" | *(remove entirely)* |
| "competitive advantage/edge" | *(remove entirely)* |
| "Ready to start your [ISO] journey?" | Rotate: resource offer, next-step checklist, diagnostic question, ROI calculator reference |
| "Whether you're a small/mid/large..." | Rotate openings: story, statistic, question, contrarian statement, scenario, pain-point hook |
| "Everything you need to know" | *(remove entirely)* |
| "Complete guide to..." | *(remove entirely)* |

### Stat Usage
- NEVER use "98% first-time pass rate, 250+ audits, 15+ years" as a group
- Pick at most ONE stat per article, frame differently each time

## Content Differentiation Rules

### Opening Patterns (Rotate — Never Reuse)
1. Story/anecdote from a specific industry
2. Surprising statistic tied to the standard
3. Direct question addressing a pain point
4. Contrarian statement challenging common belief
5. Specific scenario (e.g., "Your biggest customer just sent a letter...")
6. Pain-point hook with industry specifics

### CTA Closings (Rotate — Never Reuse)
1. Resource offer (template, checklist download)
2. Next-step checklist (3 action items)
3. Diagnostic question ("Can your team answer these 5 questions?")
4. ROI calculator reference
5. Team action item ("Share this with your quality manager")
6. Downloadable template reference

### Cost Figures (Never Copy-Paste)
- Tie costs to specific standard/industry/company size
- Vary framing: per-employee, ROI ratio, cost-per-clause, annual savings
- BANNED ranges: $8,000-$15,000, $18,000-$35,000, $3,000-$8,000
